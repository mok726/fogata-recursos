## 1. FAO (en inglés)

**Para:** copyright@fao.org
**CC:** yunga@fao.org
**Asunto:** Permission request – Spanish adaptations of YUNGA Challenge Badges on a non-commercial Scout website

> Dear FAO Copyright team, dear YUNGA team,
>
> My name is Mariano S. Cosentino. I am a Scout volunteer [and member of Scouts de Argentina – rol/grupo] and a university professor in Argentina. I run "Fogata de Recursos" (https://mok726.github.io/fogata-recursos/), a free, non-commercial website. It organises Scout and educational activities so youth leaders and teachers can search them by age group, Sustainable Development Goal and badge. There are no ads, sales or registration.
>
> The site includes activities adapted from the following YUNGA Challenge Badges. Some come from FAO's Spanish editions and others are my own translations from English:
>
> - Water Challenge Badge (2013) – 155 activities
> - Biodiversity Challenge Badge (2011) – 112 activities
> - Forests Challenge Badge (2013) – 89 activities
> - Food Security and Climate Change Challenge Badge (2009) – 87 activities
> - Climate Change Challenge Badge, 2nd edition (2016) – 86 activities
> - Nutrition Challenge Badge (2017; Spanish edition 2018) – 76 activities
> - Ocean Challenge Badge (2013) – 53 activities
> - Ending Hunger Challenge Badge (2013) – 50 activities
> - Soils Challenge Badge (2014; Spanish edition 2017) – 49 activities
> - Green Cities Challenge Badge (2025) – 40 activities
> - Pollinators Challenge Badge (2021) – 37 activities
> - Disaster Risk Reduction Challenge Badge (2020) – 36 activities
>
> Each activity is split into steps and tagged by age range and SDG. It cites FAO as the source and copyright holder and links to the original publication. Translated activities carry FAO's standard disclaimer that the translation was not created by FAO.
>
> Several of these editions predate FAO's adoption of Creative Commons licences and reserve translation and adaptation rights. For the most recent ones I could not confirm the licence. I would therefore like to request permission to:
>
> 1. publish these translations and adaptations under the same terms FAO uses for its current badges (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO), or under the equivalent CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; and
> 2. display the badge artwork of the YUNGA Challenge Badges (including Energy, Plastics, Agriculture, Gender and Governance) on the corresponding pages of the site, without using the FAO logo.
>
> If FAO prefers different conditions, a different attribution wording, or wants any content removed, I will comply promptly. A per-activity list is available on request.
>
> Thank you for producing these excellent resources.
>
> Kind regards,
>
> Mariano S. Cosentino
> [email] · https://mscosentino.com
> https://github.com/mok726/fogata-recursos
